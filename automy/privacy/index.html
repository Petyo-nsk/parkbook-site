AutoMy. — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026 · Developer: Parkbook Ltd. (Паркбоок ЕООД), UIC 208335426, Burgas, Bulgaria · support@parkbook.bg

1. The short version

AutoMy works without an account. Everything you enter — vehicles, fuel, service and expense entries, reminders, settings — is stored only on your device. We do not run a server that receives your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Vehicles (name, type, odometer, fuel type)
  • Entries (date, odometer, volume, price, amount, category, note)
  • Reminders (title, due date and/or due odometer)
  • Settings (language, units, currency, colour theme) and your Pro status

This data stays in the app's local storage on your phone. If you uninstall the app, it is deleted. You can also delete it at any time from Settings → Delete all data, and you can export it to a file from Settings → Backup to file (Pro).

3. Purchases (AutoMy Pro)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone). To check whether your purchase is active we use RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous identifier generated by the app and the purchase information from the store (product, dates, status). It does not receive your name, e-mail, vehicles or entries. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Google and Apple handle your payment details under their own policies; we never see your card number.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No account, no e-mail, no phone number
  • No location
  • No contacts, photos or files (a backup file is created only when you tap "Backup to file" and is saved where you choose)
  • No advertising identifiers, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs

5. Permissions

The app may ask for permission to show notifications so that reminders can alert you before a deadline. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in your phone's settings at any time.

6. Children

AutoMy is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

Because we do not hold your data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side — you control it fully on your device. For questions about the anonymous purchase identifier held by RevenueCat, or anything else, write to support@parkbook.bg. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

8. Changes

If we add a feature that sends data anywhere (for example optional cloud sync), we will update this policy and the app will ask you before it is enabled.
